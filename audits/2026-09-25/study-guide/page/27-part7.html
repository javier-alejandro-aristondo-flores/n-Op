Most models needed a fix that showed up only on their first real run on the card. Every fix below is recorded in the commit history.

The lesson the project drew: every model's first act on the card became a short sanity check that its validation score moves and beats its own trivial baseline, before any long run.

How the work ran

  • Models were written in parallel by separate agents, each in its own copy of the repository, and landed one at a time through a single integrator that ran the full test suite on every landing.
  • The card ran one job at a time; evaluation ran on the processor beside it.
  • The laptop lost power more than once during runs, so every stage checkpoints and resumes. A guard paused card jobs whenever the laptop went onto battery.
