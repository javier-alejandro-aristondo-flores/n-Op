Operator Suite Study Guide

Every model in the suite, from the calculations it learned from to the verdict it earned, and what the audit has confirmed since. Each part ends with checks: answer, reveal, then rate yourself honestly.

Repository state: commit 485a7d9, 17 September 2026 Last training stopped 18 September Guide written 2 October 2026, with the audit paused

How to use it
  1. Read a part. The parts follow the order the work happened: data, split, judge, train, models, then what came after.
  2. At each check, write your answer in the box, then press Show answer.
  3. Rate yourself: Knew it, Partly or Didn't know. Be strict; the map is only as useful as the ratings.
  4. Take the final quiz, then read the knowledge map. It marks the parts to reread.
Your answers and ratings stay in this browser only. Anything marked Found is a defect the audit confirmed; Pending means an auditor's claim not yet verified.
